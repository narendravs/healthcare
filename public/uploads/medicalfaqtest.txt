Question: What are the primary symptoms of sleep apnea?
Answer: The most common symptoms of sleep apnea include loud and chronic snoring, choking or gasping for air during sleep, waking up with a dry mouth or sore throat, morning headaches, and experiencing excessive daytime sleepiness or fatigue.

Question: How long should a patient fast before a routine lipid panel blood test?
Answer: For a standard lipid panel, which measures cholesterol and triglycerides, patients are typically required to fast—consuming no food or beverages except water—for 9 to 12 hours before the blood sample is drawn.

Question: What is the recommended first-aid treatment for a mild first-degree burn?
Answer: To treat a mild first-degree burn, immediately cool the burn by holding it under cold, running tap water for roughly 10 minutes. Afterwards, apply a clean aloe vera gel or moisturizer to the skin, and cover it loosely with a sterile, non-stick gauze bandage to protect the area from friction. Do not apply butter or ice to the burn.
